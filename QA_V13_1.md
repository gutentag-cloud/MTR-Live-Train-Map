## Map checks in code
- Official-map station hotspot builder skips stations whose only line is `LRL`.
- Official-map train renderer rejects `LRL` states.
- Official raster colour targets already exclude `LRL`.
- Real-map train geometry is generated from `geoPathForState()` and the train body is sliced from that same path.
- Real-map visible train footprint is hidden below zoom 15.
- Train click handlers are retained on the physical body, nose and transparent hit target.
- Real-map renderer has no GeoTD warp fallback.
- TCE Hong Kong-bound activation constant is 13 Sep 2026 00:00 HKT.

## Time checks
- Main clock/service-day logic uses `Asia/Hong_Kong`.
- EAL badge update time is formatted to HKT.
- EAL train-list update time is formatted to HKT.
- EAL detail-panel timestamp is formatted to HKT.
- Light Rail upstream update label is formatted to HKT.

## Environment limitation
The build container could serve the application locally, but outbound route/Overpass fetches were not reliably available during QA. Accordingly, syntax/server/fallback behavior was tested locally while live geographic-source integration is designed to fail safely to bundled WGS84 geometry rather than GeoTD warping.
