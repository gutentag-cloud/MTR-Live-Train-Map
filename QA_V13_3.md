## Accuracy boundaries

Heavy-rail station-board seconds are an estimate. MTR's live ETA fixes the current prediction window; WTT timing seconds and the current line-delay model refine the second phase. They are not claimed as one-second physical truth.

Light Rail positions remain ETA/WTT-inferred because the public feed does not provide vehicle GPS. On the official Light Rail schematic, the dot is guaranteed by the renderer to be snapped to a detected route-colour pixel when the raster calibration succeeds, but dense parallel route bundles can still be topologically ambiguous. The GeoTD Light Rail view remains the higher-detail track-geometry view.
