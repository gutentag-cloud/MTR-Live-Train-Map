## GeoTD detailed-track extraction
- 1,284 retained heavy-rail vector track paths
- 13,689 sampled path points
- 98 heavy-rail station control points in the GeoTD -> WGS84 transform
- station-control-point transform error: exactly 0 at the supplied anchors by construction
- maximum adjacent point interval after georeference: under 0.22 km; no multi-kilometre chord artefacts in the detailed track payload
- line-colour drawings are constrained against the validated operating GeoTD track skeleton to reject key/legend/decorative strokes
- the extracted layer retains parallel running roads, junction curves, crossovers and coloured siding/depot track where connected to the operating corridor

## Geometry consistency
- Geographic train marker: current `state.pos.x/y` -> the same GeoTD/WGS84 warp used by the detailed-track layer
- Geographic track: actual coloured GeoTD vector strokes, not station-to-station chords
- Vector schematic background and train dots: same coordinate/path table, so a dot is evaluated on the route polyline itself
- Official map: train vehicles are dots; after the same-origin official image loads, each dot is snapped to a sampled pixel belonging to its own route colour. The search expands if the coarse seed is displaced, so the rendered dot is route-on-pixel rather than floating beside the line.

## Station ETA
- station hotspots exist in GeoTD, geographic, clean schematic and official-map modes
- clicking a heavy-rail station queries `/api/station-board`
- exact upstream timestamps preserve `HH:MM:SS`

## Light Rail
- official MTR Light Rail route-map PDF is a lazy separate view
- operational train marker set is filtered to `sourceTier === live` while the Light Rail feed is fresh
- no WTT-only Light Rail vehicle is drawn; no fresh/matched ETA means no train marker
